Connect to the coach

  1. Open a recording and leave it paused.
  2. Open Interview Coach and click Capture call audio.
  3. Choose Chrome Tab → Interview replay — Coach, enable Share tab audio, then Share.
  4. Keep Capture my mic off for a recording containing both speakers.
  5. Press Play here, leaving the player unmuted. Try Automatic or Manual questions in the coach.

Compare the same section

Use 1× for normal timing. Pause or loop to inspect the UI. For a clean comparison after seeking or looping, Stop the coach, pause and rewind the recording, then capture this tab again.

A mixed recording is one interviewer audio stream. This tool does not separate the original speakers or provide a separate candidate track.

The player opens your file locally without uploading it. Sharing this tab through the coach sends its audio and transcript to your configured providers, just like a live call. Normal API charges apply.
