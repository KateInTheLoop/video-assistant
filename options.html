Interview Coach settings

Keys

Who is interviewing, and for what

One candidate (name, CV, stories, LinkedIn, notes) and one job (title, description, cover letter, company research) are active at a time; the coach uses exactly those two. Fill them in on the Content tab. A name or title typed there becomes the label here.
Keys are stored in this extension's local storage only. Transcripts never leave this machine; only the recent transcript text is sent to Claude for hints and audio goes to the speech-to-text provider for captions.

About you

The CV is the ground truth: stories are drafted from it and it is sent to Claude with every hint so it can point at your real experience. Upload a file or paste text; either way you get editable plain text here, the most token-efficient form for Claude. DOCX, ODT and text files are read locally and never leave the machine; a PDF is transcribed once by Claude (a few seconds). Old .doc files and Google Docs: download as .docx first.

Story bank

These are the only things hints will point at. Edit them until they sound like you (edits save as you type). Add stories the CV doesn't show: a failure, a conflict, a time you changed your mind.
Guided story interview Ten standard behavioral questions. Answer each the way you would say it out loud; Claude tidies it into a story card without adding anything. Skip any you have no story for.

About the job

Claude reads the job description against your story bank and lists the questions it makes likely. A ⚠ line is a gap: a probable question with no story behind it. Those are the ones to prepare; during the call a question with no matching story is flagged the same way.

    More for the coach (optional)

    Anything else the coach should know: a cover or motivation letter, your LinkedIn profile, company research, or plain notes (why this role, why you are moving, constraints, the interviewer's name). Cover letters and company research are kept with the job; LinkedIn and notes with you. It is used for motivation and logistics questions and to tailor cues to this company; it never replaces your stories. Everything here is sent to Claude with each hint, cached, and nowhere else.
    Add materials

    What the coach sees

    The context sent with every live hint, by component. Sizes are estimates (about four characters per token); click Count exactly for the API's number. The whole block is cached for an hour after the first hint, so after that each hint pays only a cache read. If the total is under the cache minimum, nothing caches and every hint pays full price: add stories or a CV.

    Backup and transfer

    Everything lives only in this browser's extension storage. Export the active candidate or job as a JSON file to move it to another machine or keep a copy; importing adds it as a new entry. The story-bank export is the older format: stories only, merged by title.
    Microphone test
    Chrome remembers the permission for this extension once granted here. If it was blocked, click the lock icon in the address bar and allow the microphone.
    Diagnostics and developer mode One row per live hint: latency, model, tokens in and out, and how many input tokens came from the prompt cache. After the first hint of a session the cache share should be high, which is what keeps hints fast and cheap.
    Adds trace points to the coach window: every caption, scheduler decision, hint request, first token, partial render and error, with millisecond offsets from the session start. In developer mode they are logged to the coach window's console (right-click the window, Inspect) and Save transcript also downloads a .trace.log next to the transcript. Off by default; it changes nothing about what is sent to Claude.
    Past sessions Each stopped session keeps its transcript, pacing per answer, and a debrief: which story you used, which STAR parts were present, one thing that went well and one to improve. Nothing here is uploaded anywhere.
